What makes this different

Real stars, not decoration
9,096 stars from the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, precessed to your instant through the same path the planets take. Not a stock star field rotated to look plausible.
The nakshatra ring
Twenty-seven sidereal sectors in Devanagari and IAST, with each planet marked at its own longitude and the Moon's nakshatra filled.
It says how it was computed
Ayanamsa, boundary scheme, calendar and timezone are printed on every render. A picture that will not say how it was made cannot be checked.
Your town is in here
Every settlement above five hundred people, under the name your family uses — Bombay finds Mumbai, and Devanagari works.
